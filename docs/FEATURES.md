# Pickfight: what already exists

Shipped-feature inventory (evidence: closed issue numbers). Read it before proposing work; frame questions as extensions of what is here.

**Maintenance rule: Every PR that adds, removes or changes a player-facing feature updates this file in the same PR.**

## Core movement and combat
- Weapon is the arm: force-driven jointed weapon, relative-vector drag aim (#1, #2, ADR-0003/0005/0006)
- Damage, blocking, knockback, ring-out deaths (#2)
- Strike damage scales with head speed: a swing under 700 px/s deals nothing, damage rises to the weapon's full value at 2200 px/s, and no single strike exceeds 90 (#2)
- Weapon heads bite into surfaces for traction; weapons and traversal made more responsive (#110, #136, #180, #181)
- A steady circular swing on flat ground always rolls the player the way it turns, for every weapon, however the thumb circles (#667): while the drag is circling and the head is over the floor, the part of the extension push that would throw the body against the circling (head planted ahead, pushing out) is faded out; a straight push down (a vault) is unchanged
- Weapon turn guard and anti-tunnelling vs terrain and other heads (#23, #82, #103, #109)
- Trapped weapon head phases home after a delay (#115)
- A long head (sword, boomstick) wedged into a wall phases free after 1.5 s, held or released, and turns solid again once its circles are out of the wall (#664)
- Spawn protection: about 1 s invulnerable, blinking (#114); a protected fighter is neither hurt nor knocked by a meteor or falling rock, like spikes and saws (#991), nor by a boomstick bullet or boomerang (#1004), nor shoved by a ghost's Boo (#1053), nor dragged by a Magnet field (#1056), nor reeled or shoved by a grapple or fishing-rod hook (#1093)
- Round winner keeps their weapon; everyone else respawns with the pickaxe (#6, ADR-0005)
- Rising lava kill zone ends stalling (#22, #45, ADR-0012)
- Hitmarkers and debug damage numbers (#33)
- Smooth physics interpolation, game-time clock, seeded match RNG (#108, #182, #187)

## Weapons
- Pickaxe: everyone's starting weapon (#2, #135, #181)
- Staff, sword, dagger (#13)
- Dagger stab bonus: a stab thrown straight out along the haft deals 1.2x damage, capped at 90 (PR #222)
- Axe: heavy, sluggish, double-sided head, 90 damage (#49, #90)
- Spear: long reach, weak up close (#272); retired from rotation, never spawns as a pickup or in random-weapons (#642)
- Shield: a wide (50 px, was 36) heater-shaped plate with a matching polygon hitbox (#465); blocks hits landing on its face, and its face is the only thing that blocks shots other players fire (#631, ADR-0024): a boomstick bullet vanishes, a grapple or fishing rod hook goes home empty and a boomerang turns back, none dealing damage; every other head, the shield's back and edges, and the holder's own shield let shots through; a bash does little damage but big knockback (#275)
- Umbrella: held overhead it slows your fall hard (a steady fall of about 85 px/s, half the old speed, #666), catches wind zones and turns hits on its canopy, falling rocks and meteors included; a short poker otherwise (#269, #569); bots open it on their own while falling (#915)
- Pogo stick (retired from the pickup rotation, #655: the code stays): auto-bounce, charge-and-release launch, damage only from stomps (#271); bouncier hop and launch, hops keep their height (#622)
- Boomstick: fires bullets on a 3 s interval, heavy knockback (#55, #92, ADR-0014). A thin ring over the holder's name tag fills toward the next shot and flashes white as it fires (skipped with Reduce flashes on; host screen only, #965). A bullet already fired keeps flying and can still hit (credited to the shooter) after the shooter is eliminated; round end or a kick clears it (#570)
- Grappling hook (#150): its hook flies up to 650 px (was 560, #653), sticks to a wall or a player and zips you toward it while you hold the drag; a player it hits takes 8 (nothing for a teammate) and feels no pull; lets go on release or a KO, no time limit (#630); the hook plays a clink when it latches (#1001)
- Flail: chain physics, boosted climb (#150, #228)
- Boomerang (#150; catching it back in the hand plays a short catch sound and throws a small white spark burst at the thrower, the burst skipped with Reduce flashes on, #862; #481/#485: also thrown along the aim by an action tap -- PC Space or left click, gamepad bumper or stick click, fired on key-up within 0.25 s -- instead of toggling release; holding the button never throws; the flick throw stays on phones, but is off on PC, Online and gamepad seats, #487)
- Fishing rod: casts a line that reels the player it hits in to you, for about 6 damage (none on a teammate); a planted head resists but still slides. The hook does not stick to the stage; the reel breaks on release, arrival, a KO, or after 1.5 s (#273, #629); the cast arcs under gravity (#625) and flies 800 px, about 0.8 s, before it retracts on its own (#641); its hook clinks when it catches a player (#1001)
- Magnet: pulls or pushes nearby players and their weapon heads; the field is blocked by solid terrain, and plays a pull sound when it grabs someone, at most once per 1.5 s; spawn-protected fighters are skipped, silently (#274, #933, #978, #1056)
- Weapons spawn as on-stage pickups; body touch swaps (#14, ADR-0009); pickups scale with player count (#152); never spawn on a spot a living player is standing on, another free spot is used instead (#333); a fighter touching a pickup of the weapon they already hold leaves it on the stage, no claim, callout or Magpie count, and it stays for anyone else or for them after a switch (#1006); a new pickup is never a weapon already lying on the stage while another offered weapon is not, the passed-over weapon kept for a later draw, and when every offered weapon is lying there it deals as before (#1038); a refilled bag never opens with the weapon that closed the last one (a one-weapon offer still repeats, seeded like the rest, #1042); host-side choice, joiners see it through the pickup snapshot
- Pickups pop in when they spawn (#816): scale up from 0.2x to 1x over 0.3 s with a small overshoot, under a one-shot gold ring that expands and fades over 0.5 s, with a soft `pickup_spawn` pluck at the spot (once per spawned pickup, heard online through the Sfx snapshot trailer, #998); motion only, so no Reduce flashes switch; collectable from the first frame
- Match point plate (#825): when a round starts and a fighter (a team, in Teams) is exactly one round win from taking the match, a "MATCH POINT: <name>" plate shows under the stage title card for 2.5 s in their colour on an ink plate (two leaders stack, up to four), and the leader's name tag keeps a small gold dot until the round ends. Classic, King of the Hill, Sudden Death, Soccer and Capture the Flag only; none in Stock, none in endless Solo practice (no scoring target), none in round 1 of a first-to-2-or-more match, and none when the match target is 1 (Soccer, Capture the Flag: everyone would be on match point from round 1, #829). Host screen only (no remote-client banner path exists for it). `scripts/MatchPointPlate.gd`, hooked from `Announcer.gd`
- Pickup callout (#818): a fighter who grabs a pickup gets the weapon's translated name floating up 40 px from above their head over 1.2 s, in their colour on an ink plate, above the name tags; one per fighter (a new grab replaces it), none for ghosts or the pickaxe; "Pickup callouts" in Settings > More options (#882, on by default, saved, put back by Restore defaults) turns them off, and pickups and the Magpie stat are unchanged
- Per-stage weapon themes: pickup odds are weighted by the parts on the stage (wind zones favour the umbrella, tall layouts the grapple, bounce pads or flat floors the pogo); weighted not exclusive, every enabled weapon can still appear, pickaxe never; optional `weapon_weight_overrides` on a stage; seeded (#310)

## Match flow and scoring
- Endless round loop over the live roster (ADR-0004, ADR-0007)
- Per-mode match target (#544): the Host panel's adjustable target row (host screen, gamepad menu and the PC remote client's host row) is labelled by the mode: "First to" N rounds in Classic, King of the Hill and Sudden Death; "Lives" in Stock (`HostSettings.stock_lives`; Stock has no rounds target, #644); "Goals to win" in Soccer (`soccer_goals`, default 3) and "Captures to win" in Capture the Flag (`ctf_captures`, default 2), both 1-10, saved in `user://audio.cfg` and set by the `soccer_goals` / `ctf_captures` host commands (the generic `target` command follows the picked mode). The Soccer and CTF count is the whole match (#646): the first team to reach it goes to the victory podium, with no rounds on top, and the remote HUD sends it. The stage title card shows the saved number; the lobby mode cards carry none. The phone controller page's host target row follows the same labels from the lobby state's `target_kind` (#576)
- Matches: first to N rounds, host picks N (except Stock, Soccer and Capture the Flag, which are one round; Soccer and Capture the Flag end on their goals or captures target, #644, #646); victory podium (#120); each phone taps Continue and the room returns to the lobby once every human has (bots excluded), after 30 s, or on a host key or left click (#337, #640); the prompt names only the Continue inputs seated: Space or click (host PC seat; an Online client continues with Space, A or a click on the host's victory screen, #976), A (gamepad), a tap on your phone, and a phone-only room keeps "Tap Continue on your phone" (#639)
- Sudden Death tiebreaker (#554, #556): one system for every tie. A round whose last players (or teams) go down on the same tick, in any mode, is no longer a draw nobody scores: those players respawn on the spot with their weapons and zero damage, under spawn protection, for a Smash-style play-off with a "Sudden Death!" banner and announcer line; one hit KOs (a hit on a shield or open canopy face is blocked, #569), the rising lava drops back and stops, and once one is left standing stray bullets are cleared (#611). After 15 s falling rocks rain over the stage ("Sudden Death!" again), faster every 5 s; a rock KOs too. Another double KO replays it among the same players, keeping the clock. At 60 s a still-tied round ends a draw: nobody scores and the announcer says "Draw!". Stock's time-limit tie is the same tiebreaker, played in place and announced "Overtime!". Players already out stay out. Not for an abandoned round or one a host kick decided. `scripts/Tiebreaker.gd`
- Teams mode: Red vs Blue, no friendly damage, team rings, auto-balance (#236, ADR-0018)
- Team letters for colour-blind players (#752): every team marker also carries the team's letter (the first character of its translated name, so R and B in English, R and A in Spanish) in ink on the team colour, as a small rounded badge: left of each team player's name tag, top right of each lobby card in a Teams match, over each team player's name on the podium, centred on each Soccer goal and each Capture the Flag base, and on each flag's cloth. The in-round scoreboard row starts with the letter in brackets ("[R] Name  RED"). A free-for-all shows none. No new shapes or patterns
- Player numbers (#809): a colour-blind option in Settings > More options (off by default, saved in `user://audio.cfg`, put back by Restore defaults). On, each living fighter's name tag carries its seat number (1 to 8) as the same ink-on-colour rounded badge as the team letters, left of the tag, and each lobby player card shows it top right; bots get numbers, ghosts none, and a team player keeps the team letter instead. Off changes nothing
- Game modes (#352): the host phone's menu picks Classic, King of the Hill, Sudden Death, Stock, Soccer or Capture the Flag (Hot Potato is retired from every picker, #645: the code stays, and a saved Hot Potato choice loads as Classic) for the whole match (in the one-hit modes, Sudden Death and Stock's overtime, a hit taken on a shield face or open canopy face is blocked and does not eliminate, #569), saved with the other host settings (`user://audio.cfg`). Combines with Free-for-all or Teams (the Format); Soccer (#402) and Capture the Flag (#403) are Teams only (greyed out in Free-for-all, and switching Teams off drops them to Classic); a forced Hot Potato round stays Free-for-all only. King of the Hill in Teams: teammates hold the hill together, a mixed hill is contested and frozen, hold time banks per team. King of the Hill is readable (#662): the first to 15 s of total hold time wins; the win shows a "<name or team> holds the hill!" banner and freezes everyone, with no eliminations; a change of holder to a different fighter (or team) also shows a "<name or team> takes the hill!" banner in the new holder's colour (#1077, through the same kill feed banner, so Online joiners see it too; none for the first holder, an emptied hill or the same holder returning); a knocked-out player respawns after 4 s (spawn protection, pickaxe) at the floored spawn farthest from the hill, keeping their hold time, and the last player standing does not end the round; a 3-minute clock shows top centre for its last 30 s and at time-out the most hold time (a team's total in Teams) wins, a tie going to overtime until someone holds the hill alone. The hill is dim gold when empty, filled in the holder's colour with a fill ring toward 15 s and a crown floating above it when held, and flashes red and white with a "CONTESTED" label when contested; the host screen's top-centre row reads "Red 6/15 · Blue 3/15" and each phone shows its own "Hill N / 15 s" (in the phone's language). Rule card: "Stand in the gold circle alone. First to 15 s total wins." The Rise runs in Classic, is off in King of the Hill and Hot Potato, and in Sudden Death starts after half the grace period at 1.5x speed. Each mode bans modifiers: Sudden Death double damage, Hot Potato weapon roulette, King of the Hill meteor shower. The stage title card shows the mode name and a one-line rule; the how-to-play panel has one card per mode. All of it is one table, `GameModes.TABLE`
- Soccer mode (#402, Teams only): one light, floaty physics ball (mass 0.2, gravity 0.75x, #674, so strikes lift it into the air easily) on a closed pitch with a goal at each end (Red defends the left, Blue the right); players bat it with weapon heads and bodies. A ball in a goal scores for the other team, with a "GOAL!" banner and a 1.5 s pause, the banner names the scorer on a second line under GOAL! (#1070: the player credited with the goal, in their colour, at 56 px, the same name the kill feed shows; an own goal or an untouched ball shows GOAL! alone; the winning goal too; Online joiners read it through the same mode HUD mirror), then the ball goes back to the centre and everyone to their own half. First team to the goals target (default 3) wins the match (#646): the winning goal ends the one round and the victory podium follows. After every other goal, play moves to a different pitch of Pitch, Dunes and Cage that is switched on (never the same one while another is on; it stays if only one is), with the score carried over: GOAL! banner and pause, then the new pitch's title card, then kick-off. Telemetry lists every pitch played. The score shows top centre. A knocked-out player respawns after 4 s (`Soccer.RESPAWN_SEC`, longer than Stock's 1.5 s so the team that got the KO has the edge, #648) with spawn protection through the same respawn Stock uses (`Respawn.gd`), so nobody sits out (each in-round return plays a soft rising `respawn` chime at the spawn point, none at round start or for a kicked slot, #1015) (a player the host kicks while waiting stays out). Neither a respawn nor a kick-off placement ever lands inside a goal; with every spawn in a goal it falls back to the own-half spawn nearest the centre (#648). The pitches are big (#649): about 1.4x the old width (a 2240x1260 view that the camera zooms out to fit, like the large stages), and the ball is light (mass 0.35, top speed 2400) so a solid swing carries it well across. Played only on the three pitches, each switchable in Soccer's own stage list (#647; never all off, the mode falls back to all three): no other stage has goals. No rising lava; meteor shower banned. Bots chase the ball, get behind it and drive it at the enemy goal. The ball thumps on hard strikes and bounces: a `ball_thump` sound at the ball, louder the harder the hit, silent for a roll, at most one per 0.15 s (#899). A ball that stays under 20 px/s for 8 s with no fighter body or weapon head within 80 px returns to the centre like a kick-off (no score, no banner; #988). A 5-minute match clock (#1012, `Soccer.TIME_LIMIT_SEC`) runs while the ball is in play and stops during the goal pause and kick-off; at time the team with more goals wins, and a level score plays the shared Sudden Death tiebreaker (#554, #556) in place, announced "Overtime!" like Stock's time-limit tie: the ball goes, goals stop counting, no respawns, the first KO wins (no golden goal). The clock shows top centre for its last 30 s as "m:ss", the same pill and rule as King of the Hill's (#1040, `ModeKit.clock_text()`), frozen with the clock during the goal pause and kick-off and gone at time-out; Online joiners see it through the same mode HUD mirror. Mode award "Top Scorer" (the goal is credited to the last player of the scoring team to touch the ball)
- Capture the Flag mode (#403, Teams only, greyed out in Free-for-all like Soccer): each team has a base with a flag on a symmetric hall (Red left, Blue right). Touching the enemy flag picks it up; the carrier keeps their weapon and can fight, but any hit (or a KO) makes them drop it. A dropped flag lies where it fell and returns home after 10 s, or at once when a player of its own team touches it; the player who dropped it cannot re-grab it for 1.5 s. Bringing the enemy flag into your own base is a capture (the flag goes home); first team to the captures target (default 2) wins the match (#646): the winning capture ends the one round and the victory podium follows. A 6-minute match clock (#1013, `CaptureTheFlag.TIME_LIMIT_SEC`) shows top centre for its last 30 s as "m:ss", the same pill and rule as King of the Hill's (#1040), and ends the round when it runs out: the team with more captures wins; a level score plays the shared Sudden Death tiebreaker (#554, #556) in place among everyone, flags home, no respawns, announced "Overtime!", exactly as Stock's time-limit tie does. The Sudden Death banner and win screen reach Online joiners through the existing #976 paths. After every other capture, a 1.5 s pause with a big "CAPTURED!" banner in the capturing team's colour and the carrier's name under it (#1072: 56 px, in their colour, the same name the kill feed shows; the winning capture shows none; Online joiners read both lines through the same mode HUD mirror), then play moves to the other hall of Bastion and Stronghold that is switched on (it stays if only one is), flags home, score carried over, with the new hall's title card. The score shows top centre. KOs respawn after 4 s (`CaptureTheFlag.RESPAWN_SEC`, #648; a respawn may land in the player's own flag zone) with spawn protection through the shared `Respawn.gd`. Both halls are big (#649): about 1.4x the old width, a 2240x1260 view that the camera zooms out to fit. Played only on Bastion and Stronghold, each switchable in Capture the Flag's own stage list (#647; never all off, the mode falls back to both): no other stage has bases. No rising lava; meteor shower banned. Bots: the lowest-slot bot on a team defends its flag, the others attack the enemy flag, everyone chases an enemy carrier, a carrier runs home, a dropped flag is run down. Mode award "Flag Runner" (most captures). Announcer lines "Capture the Flag!", "Flag taken!" and "Captured!" use stand-in UI sounds until voice clips are recorded.
- Round modifiers, about 1 round in 3 (#50, ADR-0015, #147): low gravity, heavy gravity (#840: 1.5x fall speed), heavy weapons, big heads, fast lava, slippery floor, tiny weapons, weapon roulette, meteor shower, bouncy, double damage, pickup rush (#841: weapon pickups spawn twice as often), gale (#312: a stage-wide gust over the whole view, one direction per round, calm then 1.5 s warning then gust; pushes players, the umbrella catches it)
- Stock mode (#354): one round and done, like Smash (#644): the match is that single round, so the last player (or team) standing, or the time-limit leader after the overtime tiebreak, wins the match and the victory podium follows straight away, in Free-for-all and Teams; no "first to N" applies. 1-10 lives (default 3) and a 2 / 5 / 8 / 15 min or no time limit (default 8), both set on the host phone and remembered. A lost life respawns after about 1.5 s at the spawn farthest from the others (only one with floor under it and, when another is left, not within 120 px of spikes or a saw at its current spot, #982; in Soccer and CTF only one on its own half, #605; Soccer and CTF wait 4 s instead, #648), with spawn protection, the pickaxe and zero damage; out of lives means out, with the KO ghost. No rising lava. Lives show as pips under the name tag (in the fighter's colour; a fighter on their last life shows one warning-red pip with a thin ink ring, #1064, the same on Online joiners since they run the same `NameTags.gd`; dropping to one life also shows a "<name> is on their last life!" banner and a successful steal a "<name> stole a life!" banner (#1078), each in the fighter's colour with the kill feed's name, none in overtime or for a rejected steal, replayed to Online joiners through the effect stream) and as "♥ N" on the phone. Timeout: most lives wins (a team's total in Teams); a tie plays the shared Sudden Death tiebreaker (#554, #556) in place among the tied, announced "Overtime!". In Teams an eliminated player can "Steal a life" from the team-mate with most (2+ lives). Countdown top centre, pulsing in the last 10 s. One stage per match (#375): Stock has its own multi-select stage list (#647): by default only the competitive-flagged stages are on; with one on that stage plays every match, with several one is drawn at random per match from the on ones and held for the match. Remembered per mode in `user://audio.cfg`; an old single Stock pick migrates to "only that stage on". The phone's old single-pick grid and Random tile are gone (#647): Stock's stages are picked on the Stages & Rules page (the `stock_stage` command still sets one stage as the only one on). Stock rolls no round modifiers (`no_modifiers` in `GameModes.TABLE`). Each other mode keeps its own stage on/off list (Classic, King of the Hill and Sudden Death over the general rotation, Soccer over its three pitches, Capture the Flag over its two halls), edited on the same screen
- Kill feed, KO credit, match awards (#148), including Longest airtime, the longest stretch with no body contact (#337); a hazard (spikes, saws, lava) or ring-out death credits whoever last hit the victim within 3 s of game time, else a self-KO; teammates never earn it (#311)
- REVENGE! banner (#834): a fighter who KOs the player who last KO'd them in the same round gets the centre banner in their colour; a streak or first-blood banner wins, and self-KOs, hazards and teammates never trigger it
- Base match awards (#148): Top Brawler (most KOs, else Heavy Hitter for most damage dealt), Butterfingers (most self-KOs, else Punching Bag for most damage taken) and Hard to Kill (longest alive)

- Bots dodge meteors (#985): in a Meteor shower round a Normal or Hard bot whose height a meteor's straight path will cross within about 0.8 s, within the meteor's radius plus the bot's body, runs sideways out of the path (never off a ledge: with an edge close behind or no safe side it carries on as usual); Easy bots keep standing in the strike line. Host-side input only, so online joiners see it through normal player position state
- Bots sidestep incoming boomstick bullets (#997): a Normal bot (from 400 px) or Hard bot (from 600 px) not holding a shield, with a rival's bullet closing on it along a line that will pass within 90 px, leaves the line at full input perpendicular to it (a flat shot, which has only up and down to dodge into, is hopped over instead) until the bullet is no longer on a collision course; never off a ledge (with an edge close behind or no safe side it carries on as usual), and not while hooked or mid-leap; Easy bots keep walking down the line; a shield bot still raises its face (#977). - Bots sidestep a thrown boomerang and an incoming hook (#1094): the bullet sidestep (#997) also reacts to a rival's boomerang or flying grapple or fishing-rod hook closing on a Normal bot (from 400 px) or Hard bot (from 600 px) along a line passing within 90 px, with the same rules: full input perpendicular to the line, a flat shot hopped over, never off a ledge, not while hooked or mid-leap, Easy bots never dodge, and shield and umbrella bots keep facing the shot (#977, #1014). Host-side input only, so online joiners see it through normal player and projectile state

- Bots steer clear of spikes and saws (a moving saw by its current position) and move upwind of a stage gust warning once a gust part exists (#313)
- Bots hunt deliberately: they pick the rival cheapest to reach (a rival high up on a ledge is the last chosen) and stick with it, never a teammate, and a bot hooked on a ledge by its own pickaxe sweeps the head off it instead of hanging there (#302)
- Bots no longer stall rounds (#409): a bot held at an edge for 3 s tries a wider gap (up to 150 px) so two bots either side of a pit meet; it drops a pickup it has chased for 10 s without reaching (for 90 s) and goes back to fighting; and King of the Hill's default hill (no stage hill spot) sits on the floor under the spawns' centre, not in mid-air where nobody on the ground is inside it; Reactor now has its own hill spots out on each wing of the floor, clear of the molten core (#416), so its King of the Hill rounds end by hold time
- Bots get past what used to stall them (#409): after 3 s held at an edge they also step onto moving ground (see-saws, turning sails, a crane) and keep riding it, and after 12 s waiting in one place they leap over the edge and walk through spikes and saws; one stuck against a breakable wall (Reactor's shields) backs off and chops it down; a duel where neither can reach the other (one on a ledge) is dropped for 12 s so the bot goes to its rival; and holding off a rival at an edge gives up after 6 s. 4-bot King of the Hill and Stock rounds now end on Carousel, Springboard, Gantry, Mill, Updraft, Reactor, Vent and Summit
- Bots keep their own swing from throwing them over a rival and off the stage: no closing on a rival with a drop right past it, a gentler chop near an edge, a brake when carried towards one, and no swinging while thrown into the air; and they press harder as opponents dwindle (shorter hesitation, closer fighting, further engagement, a faster vault) up to full pace with one rival left (#302)
- Bots play each mode's objective: in King of the Hill they head into the hill and fight whoever holds it; in Hot Potato the bot that is "it" chases a rival and every other bot keeps away from "it"; in Soccer they get behind the ball and drive it at the enemy goal (#402); in Capture the Flag they defend, attack, escort and chase the carrier (#403); Sudden Death (and any mode without an objective) plays as Classic (#353)

- Host goes online; remote seats send relative input (#239). Online only in an Online match since #435: a Couch match refuses remote seats (the PC client reads "That host is playing a Couch match, not Online.") and Solo has no relay at all
- PC client takes a gamepad: the first pad's right stick drives the arm (same deadzone as a host gamepad seat), and the mouse takes over again once the stick centres (#435)
- Online edges (#446): when the host's room closes (it quits or goes offline) every remote client lands on the join screen reading "Host left.", with no host migration. A remote seat that joins mid-match watches until the next round starts, then plays, taking a freed slot at 0 points exactly as a late phone does (#161). The host pings each remote seat about once a second over the seat channel; the round trip shows beside the player in the lobby (host screen and PC client) and on the scoreboard, in a warning colour above 150 ms, and a slow ping never kicks anyone
- Online host controls on the PC (#458): with Go online on, the host needs no phone. Every player card but the host PC's own seat has a Kick button, two-step like the phone's Leave game (#992): the first press reads "Tap again to kick" for 3 s, a second press inside that window kicks, and arming one card disarms any other; and mid-match the Esc menu (the Settings panel; Esc on the captured mouse frees it and opens the menu in one press) leads with Pause/Resume, End match and the player list, each row with a Kick. A kicked remote client lands on its join screen reading "The host removed you from the match." and is refused if it joins again; the host PC can kick any seat but its own. Only the host PC has host powers Online: a remote client is never host (#578), its pause, end, kick and other host frames are ignored, and its screen shows no host-only controls. These send the same commands as the host phone's menu, so pause, end and kick behave identically
- Host streams world snapshots to remote seats (#251)
- Host survives a relay blip without losing the room (#249)
- Dropped remote seat held for 30 s (#459): a remote player whose connection drops mid-match goes limp, keeps their roster entry for 30 s of game time (through round boundaries; a pause does not count down) and rejoins the same seat with the same score, name and looks. The PC client retries on its own every 1.5 s for that window. After 30 s the seat frees, even mid-round (the body leaves the round as a kicked one does, with no ban), and a later rejoin is a fresh seat at 0 points. Nothing is held in the lobby, as for phones (ADR-0007). A remote seat is dropped for silence only after 10 s (phones keep 2 s), and the client rejoins on its own after that close too (#579); a PC client that gets no snapshot for 5 s also drops to the rejoin loop, and a host relink resends the HUD and a full frame
- Online in the demo build, demo and full kept apart (#447): the demo build offers Online over room codes. A remote seat's hello says which build it is, and the host refuses the other one: a demo player joining a full game's room lands on the join screen reading "Get the full game to join this room", and a full-game player joining a demo room reads "This room is running the demo. Join it from the demo." Demo joins demo and full joins full as before
- Remote client shows the full match (#436): scoreboard, round result line, mode HUD line (King of the Hill holds, flag and goal scores, potato fuse, stock lives and clock), announcer banner, countdown, match podium with a Leave button. The host streams a small `hud` text frame (`scripts/RemoteHud.gd`, only on change, at most ~7 Hz) beside the snapshots. A removed body, projectile or pickup, or a new round or stage, goes out as a full snapshot at once so no ghost outlives one snapshot (#429)
- Remote client lobby (#518): the mouse stays free in the lobby and on the podium (captured only while a round shows), the Ready toggle mirrors the server so one click readies after a match start or victory, pad A or Start readies, and on a PC Enter readies and un-readies (#1035; ignored with the name or room field focused or the Esc menu open, a remote's Ready still never starts the match; the Ready button reads "Ready (Enter)" unless a gamepad is the active input) (#550: each press acts once even with Ready focused, and a clicked Ready holds until the host echoes it)
- Remembered Online name (#460): the PC join screen's name field is pre-filled with the last name used, saved in `user://remote_client.cfg` when you join; editing it and joining saves the new one. An empty or unusable name joins as "Player". with nothing saved yet, a Steam build pre-fills your Steam persona name instead (#538), and any name you save wins from then on. It also remembers the last room code (#907): the code is saved in the same file once a seat is granted, and a "Last room: CODE" button under the room field fills the field in one tap (it joins nothing, and is in the gamepad focus chain). It is hidden when nothing is saved, and the saved code is forgotten when that room turns out to be gone (the relay says there is no room with that code, or the host left)

Wishlist on Steam button (#793): the demo build

- Steam achievements (#689): 18 achievements (First Swing, Pick Your Fight, Rock Bottom, Lava Lamp, Tool Belt, Arsenal, Old Faithful, Hat Trick, Untouchable, Back Of The Net, Flag Runner, King Of The Hill, Last Life, Overtime, Party Of Eight, Boo!, Gone Fishing, World Tour), earned by any human seat (bots never; Solo skips Untouchable and Last Life), a PC client in the seat unlocks it on its own account, reported to Steam when it is running, otherwise a "Achievement unlocked" toast on the host screen; saved in `user://achievements.cfg`; names in all 8 languages, API names in `docs/steam-achievements.md` (`scripts/Achievements.gd`)
- Relay deployable on Fly.io (`relay/fly.toml`, `relay/Dockerfile`)
- In-game Feedback button in the host Settings panel: sends text (plus build, OS, stage) to the relay, which files a `needs-triage` + `feedback` GitHub issue using the relay-only `GITHUB_FEEDBACK_TOKEN`; 5 per IP per hour, 2000 chars, offline (503) until the token is set (#262)
- Anonymous match telemetry (#372): at match end the host sends one record (per-weapon damage, hits and KOs by real players, mode, stages, format, length, winner weapon, and `completed` plus `rounds_played`; no names, colours, devices or IDs) to the relay, which appends it to a JSONL file with the time rounded to the hour and never stores or logs the IP; 30 per IP per hour, 8 KB cap. A match that ends without a winner (host returns to the lobby mid-match, or the app quits, with a short wait so quitting never hangs) sends the same record with `completed` false, `rounds_played` and no winner weapon, but only after at least one round finished or the match had been live for 60 s of play (#643; Stock, Soccer and Capture the Flag are a single round, so `rounds_played` may be 0). Never sent from scenario or `--bots` runs or with the toggle off (#461: "Share anonymous match stats", last in Settings > More options, on by default), and never before a one-time notice has been seen (#617): on first launch the title or lobby shows a themed card saying what is sent and where to change it, with "Got it" (keep sharing) and "Turn off" (unticks the toggle); it works by mouse, keys and gamepad (View then A), shows once, and never appears mid-round. `tools/summarise_stats.py` prints completed and abandoned match counts separately (abandoned ones still count toward damage and hits), damage per hit, win rate by weapon, mode popularity and average match length. Needs a Fly volume (`PICKFIGHT_STATS_PATH`, see `relay/fly.toml`) to persist
- Opt-out crash reports (#681): on a release build with the Sentry Godot SDK installed (`tools/fetch_sentry.sh`, git-ignored `addons/sentry/`) and a DSN in the `pickfight/sentry_dsn` project setting, a crash sends its stack, the build version (`pickfight@<tag>`), platform, environment (`release` or `demo`) and mode and stage tags to Sentry, and nothing else: no names, room codes, IPs, logs, breadcrumbs or screenshots. "Send crash reports" in Settings > More options (default on, saved with the other host settings) turns it off from the next launch; the first-launch telemetry notice mentions it. Never starts in the editor, headless, scenario or `--bots` runs, or with an empty DSN. `--crash-test` sends a test message then crashes, for checking a build (`docs/crash-reporting.md`).

## Dev tooling
- Headless scenario runner and suite (`tools/scenario_runner.gd`, `tools/list_scenarios.sh`) with shared-state resets and parallel-safe ports (#73, #179)
- CI runs the scenario suite on every PR, `--fixed-fps 60` (#186, #195)
- Stage rotation data check: a scenario reads every stage's `mode_weights` and `weapon_weight_overrides` and fails naming the stage and key on a typo, a non-positive weight, or a dedicated-stage mode with no stage (#801)
- Scenario loads and instantiates every scene under `scenes/` and flags dead `ext_resource` paths (#802)
- Screenshot capture tools for stages and damage numbers
- Doc path check: a scenario fails, naming doc, line and path, when FEATURES, `docs/agents/`, ADRs or `docs/steam-*.md` name a repo path in backticks that does not exist (#803)
- Launch copy count check: a scenario counts stages, weapons, languages and the player cap from the game and fails, naming file and line, when site/index.html or the store-page docs quote a different number; a second scenario proves it flags a wrong count (#797)
- Controller offline check: a scenario reads `controller/index.html` and fails, naming the line, if it loads a script, stylesheet, image, font, `url()`, `@import`, fetch/WebSocket or import from a literal remote host (tappable links are allowed); a second scenario proves it flags a CDN reference (#798)
- Site link check: a scenario parses `site/index.html` and `site/press.html` and fails, naming file and line, if a relative `src` or `href` points at a file that is not under `site/`, a `#id` link matches no id on the page, or an `<img>` has no `alt`, `width` or `height`; a second scenario proves it flags a missing file and a missing alt (#799)
- Social preview check: a scenario parses the og:/twitter: meta tags in `site/index.html` and `site/press.html` and fails, naming file and line, if og:title or og:description is missing, og:image and twitter:image differ, the image does not map onto a file under `site/`, is not a PNG or JPEG, or its real pixel size differs from og:image:width/height; a second scenario proves it flags a missing file and a size mismatch (#864)
- Instant replay: F9 saves the last ~10 s (12 fps, 256x144, ~13 MB ring) as a PNG sequence plus an animated `clip.gif` (pure-GDScript encoder, worker thread, loops, 12 fps) in `user://clips/` with a toast showing the path (#329, ADR-0020, #502); only the newest 20 clips are kept, older ones are deleted on save (#609)
- Auto-saved match clip (#795): when a match ends on a credited KO, the host saves the replay buffer like F9 (PNG sequence plus `clip.gif`, same `user://clips/` folder, newest 20 kept) as soon as the final-KO slow motion finishes, and the victory screen shows a small "Clip saved" line bottom right. A "Auto-save match clips" checkbox in Settings > More options turns it off (on by default, persists, Restore defaults turns it back on); nothing is saved when no frames are buffered
- Local balance log: at each match end the host appends one JSON line to `user://balance_stats.jsonl` (Godot's user data folder) with damage and hits per weapon by real players; bots and the lobby sandbox excluded, never networked (#316); a match that ends without a winner after 1+ rounds adds a line with `"completed": false` and `"rounds_played"` (#643)
