## One-line hook

Eight players, one screen, no controllers: scan a QR code and fight with your phone.

## Short description

PickFight is a same-room party brawler for up to 8 players. No controllers needed: everyone scans a QR code and fights with their phone, swinging a pickaxe, sword or grappling hook across 43 stages. Play on the couch, online by room code, or solo against bots.

## About

PickFight is a same-room platform fighter for up to eight players. One computer (or a Steam Deck) runs the shared screen. Everybody else joins by scanning a QR code with the phone in their pocket. There is no app to install and no account to make.

Your thumb drags the weapon, and the weapon is your arm. Swing it, plant it in a wall, haul yourself up a cliff, and knock your friends into the lava. Movement is physics, not buttons. It is scrappy and a bit clumsy on purpose, and it is funny to watch.

## Features

- Up to 8 players on one screen, joined by QR code from any phone browser. No app, no controllers.
- Your weapon is your arm. Drag to swing, climb and fling yourself around.
- 43 rotating stages with lava, spikes, saws, moving platforms, bounce pads and wind.
- 13 weapons in rotation. Everyone starts with the pickaxe, and the rest drop on the stage as pickups: staff, sword, dagger, axe, shield, umbrella, boomstick, grappling hook, flail, boomerang, fishing rod, magnet.
- 6 game modes: Classic, King of the Hill, Sudden Death, Stock, Soccer and Capture the Flag, with Free-for-all or Red vs Blue teams.
- Round modifiers now and then, such as low gravity, double damage, meteor shower and slippery floors.
- Couch, Online by room code, or Solo against bots on Easy, Normal or Hard. A match is Couch or Online, never mixed.
- Gamepads work too, with Steam Deck as the host.
- Hats, eye styles and colours, picked on the phone.
- 18 Steam achievements.
- Slow-motion final KO and a "play of the round" replay.
- Comfort options: screen shake off, reduce flashes, bigger name tags, colour-blind-safe colours.
