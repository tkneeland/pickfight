## Devlog 1: Eight players, one screen (January 2027)

- **Event type:** general announcement
- **Title:** Devlog 1: how eight people fight on one screen with no controllers
- **Subtitle:** Scan the QR code and your phone becomes the weapon.
- **Image:** `screenshots/03-lobby.png` (the lobby with the join QR and eight player cards)

**Body:**

The question we get most: how do eight people play with no controllers?

The host computer runs the game and shows the shared screen. Each player scans the QR code on screen with a phone and opens a web page. There is nothing to install. Your thumb drags the weapon, and the weapon is your arm.

- Hats, eye styles and colours are picked on the phone before you ready up. Bots can fill any empty seat.
- Gamepads work too, including a Steam Deck as the host.
- If your Wi-Fi blocks phones from reaching the host, a "Phones can't join?" help popup lists the usual causes, and phones can join over the internet instead.
- Prefer separate rooms? Online lets everyone join from their own computer with a room code. A match is Couch or Online, never mixed.

We are playtesting this in February. Wishlist to hear when it opens: TODO (store page URL).

## Devlog 2: Soccer and Capture the Flag (March 2027)

- **Event type:** general announcement
- **Title:** Devlog 2: Soccer, with pickaxes
- **Subtitle:** Two team modes and the pitches and halls we built for them.
- **Image:** `screenshots/05-soccer.png` (or the `soccer` clip cut to 6 to 8 seconds). Second image: `screenshots/06-capture-the-flag.png`.

**Body:**

PickFight has six game modes: Classic, King of the Hill, Sudden Death, Stock, Soccer and Capture the Flag. This month, the two team modes.

- **Soccer:** a light, floaty ball on a closed pitch with a goal at each end. Bat it with your weapon head or your body. First team to the goals target wins, and the pitch changes after each goal.
- **Capture the Flag:** each team has a base and a flag. Grab theirs, bring it home. Any hit makes the carrier drop it, so the fight happens over the flag.
- Both are played in Red vs Blue teams, on stages built just for them.
- Bots play them too, so you can practise with fewer than eight friends.

## Devlog 3: Play of the round (April 2027)

- **Event type:** general announcement
- **Title:** Devlog 3: every round gets a replay
- **Subtitle:** Slow-motion replays, a podium, and Steam Timeline markers.
- **Image:** `screenshots/09-podium.png` (or the `podium` clip)

**Body:**

Good chaos should be shared. Here is what happens when a round ends.

- **Play of the round:** the best moment replays in slow motion over the shared screen. A multi-KO beats a hazard KO, which beats a long-range KO. Any player can skip it, and a setting turns it off.
- **Final KO:** the match-winning hit plays in slow motion with the camera punched in, then the victory podium and awards appear.
- **Awards and stats:** a table of KOs, damage and favourite weapon for every player, plus awards like Magpie and a mode award such as Top Scorer.
- **Steam Timeline:** with Steam running, every KO, round win and match win drops a marker on your Timeline, so you can find the clip later.

The demo comes in June. Wishlist so you do not miss it: TODO (store page URL).
