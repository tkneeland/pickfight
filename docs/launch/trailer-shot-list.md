**DRAFT: needs owner approval.** Nothing here is final. The owner edits the trailer from this list (owner decision, Oct 18). Facts come from `docs/FEATURES.md`, `store-page.md` and `owner-decisions.md`. Issue #728.

## Brief

- **Length:** 60 to 75 seconds. Target 70.
- **Due:** Steam pulls trailers on May 3 2027 for Next Fest (Jun 14 to 21). A placeholder cut is needed for the Coming Soon page in mid-Dec 2026.
- **One message:** eight friends, one screen, no controllers. Your phone is the pad and your weapon is your arm.
- **Rules from the playbook:** open on gameplay, not on a logo. Change the picture every 2 to 3 seconds. Show play, not counts. Keep it dialogue-free so captions work in every language.
- **Title:** the text is "PickFight", in the all-caps logo.
- **Price and date on the end card:** $9.99, Thu Jul 22 2027, Windows, macOS, Linux.

## How to capture

- **Screen recorder first.** Use OBS (or similar) at 1920x1080, 60 fps, with the game window full screen. Capture each beat as a long take and cut it down later.
- **Instant replay (F9) finds moments, it does not make final footage.** F9 saves the last 10 seconds as a 12 fps, 256x144 clip in `user://clips/` (#329). That is too small for the trailer. Use it to find a great moment, then recreate it on the recorder. It is fine for GIFs and short clips.
- **Bots fill the room.** In the lobby, the Host panel's Bots row (key B) adds bots, and the skill button picks Easy, Normal or Hard (#445, #682). The title screen's Solo choice starts a match with bots and no phones (#435).
- **Real phones for the join shot.** Film the phones with a second camera or phone, because bots do not scan anything.
- **Stage names** are on the title card at the start of each round. Use the stage list below to pick them.
- **Pickups.** Weapons other than the pickaxe drop onto the stage. Replay the round until the one you want drops, or run a round with the weapon roulette modifier.
