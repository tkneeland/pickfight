PICKFIGHT

Eight players, one screen, no controllers: scan a QR code and fight with your phone.

Launching July 22, 2027 · Windows, macOS, Linux · by SQL Monkey Studios

Scan, swing, shove

PickFight is a same-room platform fighter for up to eight players. One computer (or a Steam Deck) runs the shared screen. Everybody else joins by scanning a QR code with the phone already in their pocket. No app to install, no account to make.

Your thumb drags the weapon, and the weapon is your arm. Swing it, plant it in a wall, haul yourself up a cliff, and knock your friends into the lava. It is scrappy and a bit clumsy on purpose, and funny to watch.

What you get

43 stages

Lava, spikes, saws, moving platforms, bounce pads, wind and falling rocks.

6 game modes

Classic, King of the Hill, Sudden Death, Stock, Soccer and Capture the Flag, Free-for-all or Red vs Blue.

Play your way

Couch with phones and gamepads, Online by room code, or Solo against bots on Easy, Normal or Hard.

Made to be welcoming
